- routine p50/p95 (s): nearest-rank percentiles of the wall times of FR41 lines sent with think=false

- pass bar: final status in {complete, partial}; total_wall_s <= budget_s + 60 (the "small margin";

  pages >= 3; unresolved citations empty. `pass` true only if all hold.
