TASK M19b-T2 — Thinking off, output cap and non-thinking sampling on routine research steps; thinking on for plan and write; thinking text detected (FR42 request shape)

Routing: tier Mid, model sonnet, reason_code ORDINARY_IMPLEMENTATION — per-step request fields in
one module plus settings and tests; ordinary implementation with an HTTP-level test oracle, no
concurrency.

Goal:
Each deep research model step sends the request shape FR42 specifies. Brief ("brief"), query
proposal ("queries"), page choice ("pages"), note-taking ("notes") and gap check ("gap") are
thinking-off steps: top-level `think: false`, `options.num_predict` (200 by default, 800 for
"notes"), and non-thinking sampling `options.temperature 0.7, top_p 0.8, top_k 20`. Plan ("plan")
and write ("write") are thinking-on steps: `think: true`, streamed, and never carry `num_predict`
(nor the routine sampling). Every step still sends its `format` schema, `options.num_ctx` and
`keep_alive: -1` exactly as before. A thinking-off step whose reply nevertheless carries
`message.thinking` text is detected and logged, and its result is used only if its `content`
validates (thinking text is never parsed as the answer).

- FR42 (.harness/requirements.md lines 322-332). Read it in full.
- FR35 format/num_ctx rules (search requirements.md for "[FR35]").
- The edge case "A thinking-off step comes back with thinking text anyway" (requirements.md ~line 631-632).
- Milestone criterion M19b-AC2 (.harness/milestones.md, section "## M19b").
