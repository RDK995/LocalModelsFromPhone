TASK M19e-T1 — Every open reply stream carries an SSE comment keep-alive at least every 15 s (FR45 server keep-alive, FR40 amended, M19e-AC2)

Routing: tier Mid, model sonnet, reason_code ORDINARY_IMPLEMENTATION — one timer owned by each SSE
response in one function (sseResponse in server/src/http/server.ts); clear HTTP-level oracle; the
lifecycle (start on open, clear on close/cancel) is small and local.

Goal:
While a reply stream is open (POST /v1/chat's streamed response AND GET /v1/generations/{id}/events,
for deep research, ordinary web and switch-off replies alike), the server writes an SSE comment line
(e.g. `: keep-alive\n\n`) at least every `keepAliveMs` (default 15000 ms). The comment carries no
`id:` and no `event:`/`data:` field, so FR9/FR38 Last-Event-ID resume and no-gaps/no-duplicates are
unchanged. The timer is cleared when the stream closes (generation done) or is cancelled (client
gone); no enqueue after close may throw out or leak.
