Defect (found by the orchestrator reading react-native's KeyboardAvoidingView, not by a test):
mobile/node_modules/react-native/Libraries/Components/Keyboard/KeyboardAvoidingView.js
computes the overlap as `frame.y + frame.height - (keyboardFrame.screenY - keyboardVerticalOffset)`
where `frame` comes from onLayout, i.e. relative to the parent, not the window. Setup and
Settings still show the Stack header (mobile/src/app/_layout.tsx), so their KeyboardAvoidingView
starts one header-height below the top of the window while M1-C9 set
`keyboardVerticalOffset` to 0 (or left it unset). The padding therefore falls short by the header
height (~100pt on a notched iPhone), leaving the bottom of the view — where Continue / Save /
Cancel sit — up to that much under the keyboard. Chat is NOT affected: its Stack header is
hidden and its KeyboardAvoidingView sits inside SafeAreaView; leave chat.tsx alone.
