Required format (fixed decision, do not vary):
For an assistant message with a non-empty `sources` array, the content sent in history is
  `<content>\n\nSources:\n[<n>] <title> — <url>` one line per source, in stored order,
where `<n>` is the source's `n` when present, otherwise its 1-based position in the list.
Messages without sources (user messages, non-web replies) are sent exactly as today. Put the
mapping in a small exported pure function in conversationSession.ts (e.g. `historyContent(message)`)
so it is unit-testable. Do not add `steps` or `thinking` to history.
