In terms of calendar lifespan, LFP batteries maintain functional capacity well beyond 15 years, retaining roughly 70–72% of their original nameplate capacity without a sudden failure point [3]. Furthermore, LFP chemistry exhibits superior thermal safety, featuring a high thermal runaway onset threshold of 518°F (270°C) which eliminates self-sustaining fire propagation [4]. NMC batteries, however, maintain superior performance in these conditions; they retain 65–75% of their capacity at -40°C compared to LFP's lower figures [4]. Additionally, NMC is generally considered better for cold climates because it experiences less range loss, charges faster, and provides more reliable state-of-charge data than LFP under freezing conditions [5]."}
